Support

Stuck, found a bug, or need to report a player? Email support@lanternhallvr.com. Include your in-game name, your headset (Quest 3 or 3S) and what happened. Screenshots or a short clip help a lot.

Frequently asked

How do I play with friends?

At the Fellowship Board in Lanternhall, choose Private Hall. Your hall gets a six-rune code. Friends choose Join by Code and enter it. Or everyone presses Quick Play to join an open hall. The same menu is on your wrist.

How do we start the quest together?

Walk to the Cinderkeep gate and stand on the gate stone together. Up to four of you go in as a party, into your own quest room, and you come back to Lanternhall together afterwards.

The board says it can't connect.

Check that your headset is online, then press Sign in again on the network page of your wrist menu. The board's corner shows the reason if it still fails. Please include that message if you email us.

Can I play seated, or with my left hand?

Yes. Open your wrist menu, go to Comfort, and choose standing or seated, snap or smooth turning, and your sword hand. Redo Setup on the same page walks you through the first-time setup again.

How do I mute or report someone?

Open your wrist menu's Fellowship tab (or poke a name on the Fellowship Board) and poke the player. Their card has Mute (you stop hearing them, only for you), Block (their voice, emotes and name are hidden from you, and you won't be matched into their halls), Report and, if you are the hall's host, Show the Door (they leave and can't come back to that hall for 15 minutes). Nobody is told that you muted or blocked them.

A report is kept on your headset and, where available, sent to us at once. You can also send it yourself from the Settings Book's Safety page: it opens a page that lets you copy it or email it to support@lanternhallvr.com. Please don't post reports in public channels. Meta's own reporting tools on your headset work too.

Is my progress saved?

Your level, unlocks and settings are saved on your headset. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them.
