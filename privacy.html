Privacy Policy

Lanternhall · Effective November 27, 2026

This policy covers Lanternhall on Meta Quest (VR), Lanternhall on Roblox, this website (lanternhallvr.com) and our support email. It explains what information we handle, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Lanternhall is made and run by Nicholas Scheetz, doing business as Lanternhall Games ("we" or "us"). We are responsible for the information described here. Contact: support@lanternhallvr.com.

The short version

  • No ads. We never sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • No accounts or passwords with us. We never ask for your real name, address, birthday or payment details. Purchases are handled by the platform (Roblox or Meta).
  • We don't record voice chat.
  • What we keep is mostly game progress, tied to your platform's player ID or a random one.

Lanternhall on Meta Quest (VR)

  • Player name. The name you choose in the game is shown to other players in the same hall or quest. Names are filtered for offensive words.
  • Anonymous player ID. To play online, the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services, which gives your copy of the game a random player ID. We don't link it to your name or your Meta account.
  • Multiplayer connection data. When you join a hall or quest, Unity's multiplayer services (Lobby, Relay and Distributed Authority) process connection details such as your IP address, hall codes and session details, and the in-game state (positions, actions) needed to keep players in sync.
  • Voice chat. If you allow microphone access, your voice is sent live to the other players in your hall or party through Unity's Vivox service. We don't record it. You can deny the microphone and still play.
  • On your headset. Your progress, unlocks, avatar look, settings and records are stored only on your headset. The game also keeps a small technical log there to help diagnose connection problems. It is not uploaded.
  • Player reports. A report holds the reason you chose, the time, the game version, where it happened (hub, quest or Pit, the hall code and session), how many players were there, and the in-game name and anonymous player ID of you and of the player you reported. It never holds voice recordings. The full report is kept on your headset. Where available, the game also files it with Unity's Moderation service, which receives only your anonymous player ID, the reported player's anonymous player ID and the reason. The full report reaches us only if you send it yourself, by email or as a direct message to a moderator (the report page on this website reads it in your browser and does not send it anywhere).
  • Hall news. The game reads a small public notice file (notice.json) from this website when it starts and every few minutes while you play. Nothing about you is sent with it beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address, seen by GitHub Pages, which hosts this site). Offline, it is skipped.
  • Engine data. Unity Analytics and Unity's cloud crash reporting are turned off in the game. The Unity engine itself may still send Unity basic anonymous technical data, such as your device's hardware details.
  • Meta. The game is sold through the Meta Horizon Store, so Meta's terms and privacy policy cover your Meta account, your purchase and the store. The game doesn't use Meta's platform features yet. When we add them (checking that you own the game or an add-on, buying add-ons, achievements), Meta will share your Meta user ID and what you own or have unlocked with the game so those features work, and we will update this policy.

Lanternhall on Roblox

  • Your Roblox account. You play with your Roblox account. Like every Roblox experience, the game receives your Roblox user ID, username and display name from Roblox. We never see your email, phone number, birthday, password or payment details.
  • Your save. Kept in Roblox's own storage (DataStores) under your Roblox user ID: coins, XP and level, unlocks and cosmetics, settings, your emote wheel, your best times and last five victories (with the display names of that party), weekly deeds, which badges and one-time notices you've had, and the purchase IDs of shop items already given to you (so a purchase is never granted twice).
  • Leaderboards. The hub's boards (the Swiftest Fellowships, the Ember Pit ladder and the Weekly Pit Trial) keep the display names and Roblox user IDs of the players on each row, with the time or wave reached and the date. Other players can see the names. A row stays until faster runs push it off the board; each week's Trial board is kept so its top players can be given their reward.
  • Halls. While a hall is open, its name (such as "Ana's Hall"), code, player count and game version are listed in Roblox's temporary storage (MemoryStore) so others can find it. The listing expires soon after the hall closes.
  • Chat, voice, blocking and reporting. Text chat, voice chat (where Roblox allows it), blocking and reporting are Roblox's own features, run by Roblox under its rules. The game reads your Roblox block list only to hide blocked players' names from you.
  • Kicks and bans. Bans use Roblox's own ban system: Roblox stores the ban and its reason. A kick just removes a player from the server.
  • The letterbox. If you write to us with the in-game letterbox ("Word to the Makers"), your words first pass through Roblox's text filter. We receive the filtered words, the kind of letter (bug, idea or other), the game and place version, the server ID, where in the game you were, the kind of hall, how many players were there, and your device type (touch, gamepad or keyboard) and screen size. A letter carries only a pseudonymous tag for you: a one-way hash of your Roblox user ID that lets us group one player's letters but can't be turned back into your user ID or profile. Letters travel through our relay on Cloudflare and are kept as items in a private GitHub repository, with a short alert in a private Discord channel. If GitHub is unavailable, the letter goes to that Discord channel and to our email (sent with Resend) instead. Letters are deleted after 12 months.
  • Analytics. We use Roblox's built-in analytics to see how the game is going: how far Ember Pit runs get and how they end (the mode and party size), and which steps of the first-time tutorial new players reach. Roblox processes this and shows us the results in its Creator Dashboard. Test runs are not counted.
  • Badges and purchases. Badges are awarded through Roblox. Game passes and items in the Lantern Shop are bought with Robux through Roblox, which handles the payment and tells the game what you bought.
  • Hall news. The game's servers read the same public notice file from this website. Nothing about you is sent.

This website

  • This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, and may keep them in its logs.
  • The site's lettering comes from Google Fonts, so your browser also asks Google's servers for it, which sends Google your IP address and browser details.
  • Our domain's DNS is provided by Cloudflare.
  • We use no cookies, no analytics and no trackers of our own on this site.
  • Links to YouTube, Discord, Roblox and Meta take you to their sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Support email

Mail to support@lanternhallvr.com is received by Cloudflare Email Routing and delivered to our inbox, which Google (Gmail) hosts. Our replies are sent through Resend. We keep what you send us (your address, your message and anything attached) as long as we need it to handle your request, and then for up to 12 months.

Steam (coming later)

We plan a PC version on Steam. If it ships, Valve will share your Steam account ID and Steam name with the game so it can save your progress and show your name to other players, and will store your Steam achievements and stats. Purchases there are handled by Valve. We will update this policy before it launches.

Why we use it

  • To run the game you chose to play: saves, multiplayer, voice chat, leaderboards and purchases.
  • To keep players safe: reports, blocks, kicks and bans.
  • To fix and improve the game: letters, support email and the totals from Roblox's analytics.

Under the EU and UK GDPR, our legal bases are performing our agreement with you (running the game), our legitimate interests in safety, fixing bugs and improving the game, and your consent for the microphone, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes it

  • Meta: the Quest headset and the Meta Horizon Store (privacy policy).
  • Roblox: the Roblox platform, saves, leaderboards, chat, badges, purchases and analytics (privacy policy).
  • Unity: the Quest version's sign-in, multiplayer, Vivox voice chat and Moderation, and the game engine (privacy policy).
  • Cloudflare: the letterbox relay, our domain's DNS and incoming email (privacy policy).
  • GitHub: hosts this website and keeps letterbox letters in a private repository (privacy statement).
  • Discord: letterbox alerts in our private channel, and any message you send us there (privacy policy).
  • Resend: sends our email replies and backup copies of letters (privacy policy).
  • Google: Google Fonts on this website and our support inbox (privacy policy).

These companies may process information in the United States and other countries. We share information only with them, only for the purposes above, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Letterbox letters: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Support email: as long as we need it to handle your request, then up to 12 months.
  • Roblox saves and leaderboard rows: until you ask us to delete them, or Roblox tells us your account's data must be erased (for example, after you delete your Roblox account).
  • Hall listings on Roblox: gone within an hour of the hall closing.
  • Quest data on your headset: until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Your anonymous player ID and any report filed with Unity are kept by Unity under its own policy; ask us and we will request their deletion.

Children

  • Roblox: the Roblox version is open to players of all ages, including under 13, through Roblox's own accounts, age checks and parental controls. We collect no personal information directly from children beyond what Roblox provides to every experience: the Roblox user ID (and display name) is used in the game for saves and boards, and letterbox letters carry only a pseudonymous tag and Roblox-filtered words. The game never asks players for their real name, contact details or photos.
  • Meta Quest: the Quest version is for teens and adults and follows Meta's age requirements for Meta accounts. It is not directed to children under 13.
  • Parents: email support@lanternhallvr.com to ask what we hold about your child or to have it deleted. For Roblox, tell us your child's Roblox username so we can find their save.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email support@lanternhallvr.com. We may ask for something that shows the account is yours, and we will answer within a month.

  • Deleting your data on Roblox: deleting your Roblox account makes Roblox send us an erasure request, and we then delete your save and your leaderboard rows. You can also email us your Roblox username. Your letters don't carry your user ID, but if you give us your username we can find them by their tag and delete them too.
  • Deleting your data on Quest: uninstall the game or clear its data to remove everything on your headset, and email us to have data tied to your anonymous player ID deleted.
  • EU and UK: under the GDPR you also have the right to restrict or object to our use of your information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
  • US states: if your state gives you privacy rights (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Texas), you can ask to know, access, correct and delete your information, and you can use an authorized agent. We don't sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to profile you, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.
  • Choices in the game: play offline or deny the microphone on Quest to limit what is shared, and change your Quest player name at any time.

Keeping it safe

Service keys are kept as secrets, never in the games. Letters are kept in a private repository, and players' words are filtered before they leave Roblox. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold small.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
