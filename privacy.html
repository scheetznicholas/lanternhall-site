Privacy Policy

Lanternhall, by Lanternhall Games · Effective November 2026

This policy explains what information Lanternhall (the VR game for Meta Quest) uses, why, and who handles it. We keep it short because we collect very little. Questions: support@lanternhallvr.com.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No accounts or passwords with us. We never ask for your real name, address or payment details.
  • We don't record your voice chat.

Information the game uses

  • Player name. The name you choose in the game is shown to other players in the same hall or quest. Names are filtered for offensive words.
  • Anonymous player ID. To play online, the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services, which gives your copy of the game a random player ID. It is not linked to your name or Meta account by us.
  • Multiplayer connection data. When you join a hall or quest, Unity's multiplayer services (Lobby, Relay and Distributed Authority) process connection details such as your IP address, session codes and the in-game state (positions, actions) needed to keep players in sync.
  • Voice chat. If you allow microphone access, your voice is sent live to the other players in your hall or party through Unity's Vivox service. You can deny the microphone and still play.
  • On your headset. Your progress, unlocks, avatar look and settings are stored only on your headset. The game also keeps a small technical log on the headset to help diagnose connection problems. It is not uploaded.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. The Unity engine may send anonymous crash and performance diagnostics to Unity to help fix bugs.

Who processes it

Online features are provided by Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services and Vivox). The game is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store, so Meta's own terms and privacy policy apply to your Meta account and the store. See Unity's privacy policy and Meta's privacy policy.

Children

Lanternhall is intended for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, contact us and we will help remove any associated data we are able to.

Your choices

  • Play offline, or deny the microphone, to limit what is shared.
  • Change your player name at any time in the game.
  • Uninstall the game or clear its data to remove everything stored on your headset.
  • Email us to ask about, or request deletion of, any data tied to your anonymous player ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date above.
