The quest

Four friends, one keep

Team up with up to three friends. Swords, shields, bows and torches, real hand-to-hand VR combat, and a party that falls if you don't watch each other's backs.

A castle full of puzzles

Turn the boiler valves, ride the Belfry lift, ring the great bell and solve the Gallery of Kings, about half an hour of handcrafted rooms from the Gatehouse to the throne.

The Emberking

A three-phase boss fight. Stagger him, strike the crown, dodge his turrets and win the Emberking's Crown for your avatar.

The First King

In the Gallery of Kings a giant stone king wakes, crushes his own statues and turns on you. Break his glowing joints, climb his back when he kneels and strike the rune.

🔥 The Ember Pit

Step through the iron gate in the Main Hall and survive wave after wave of the keep's fiercest: Cindershanks, archers, bats, knights, gold turrets and a boss every fifth wave. Clear 15 waves free; the Pit Masters board remembers the bravest.

Your Lantern Folk

Dress your avatar in the Robing Room: hair, hats, tunics, colours and frames. Play on Normal, or swear the Oath for one-hit Hard mode and Ironheart rewards.
