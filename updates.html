🥽 Meta Quest🎮 Roblox

v0.21.0 · The Lamplighter and the Bearer's Guide Beta

2026-11-27

  • The Lamplighter has arrived: the hall's keeper now stands at his desk in the atrium, turning to look at bearers as they pass. His voice is on the way
  • The Bearer's Guide: three big books in the atrium's corner by the Hall of Glories. Foes, Great Foes, and a Handbook of the hall, from hearts and oaths to the Pit and fair play
  • Great Foes keep their secrets sealed until you've met them, or until you choose to break the seal yourself
  • The guide is on lanternhallvr.com too, with boss tactics folded away so nothing is spoiled
  • Bigger text sizes now fit everywhere: the Robing Board, the Pit and Fellowship boards, and level-up messages
  • Plus fixes: hall sign emoji no longer go missing, the welcome board has a proper stand, and the hall got a new rug, a ledger and tidier shelves

v0.20.0 · Fair Play Beta

2026-11-26

  • Party stones no longer wait forever for one missing bearer: once most of the party is on the stone, a short countdown starts and brings everyone along
  • If someone takes their headset off or goes idle, they're marked Away so the rest of the party can carry on, and they're sent home if they stay away too long
  • The fellowship can now vote: ask to lead everyone home, or part ways with a bearer who's spoiling the fun. Most of the party has to agree
  • Keys, seals and bell-weights can't be hoarded: if one bearer holds them too long without using them, they slip away and go back where they belong
  • Stuck on the two-lever puzzle or the Forge gauge? After a minute an old latch catches, so the first half holds fast and you no longer have to time it perfectly together
  • Hall hosts can Show the Door once per run in quests, and lots of fixes to text, signs and toasts

v0.19.0 · Safe Roads and Good Company Beta

2026-11-25

  • No more long black screen on solo trips: going to a quest or the Pit and coming home now takes a few seconds, with a loading card instead of darkness
  • Parties travel together faster and more reliably, to quests and back home
  • Turning a lore book page by hand is much easier: grab anywhere on the outer edge, then flick it or lift it past a third of the way. A missed grab never turns a page
  • The First King's stomp warning now shows a glowing edge and a ring that sweeps outward, so you can see where to run and when
  • Poke any player for Mute, Block and Report, and hall hosts can Show the Door. Name plates show who's who, and the Crier's Board brings hall news
  • A real treasure hoard for the First King, a carved key pedestal in the Undercroft, and lots of text and layout fixes

v0.18.0 · The Herald's Dial Alpha

2026-11-22

  • Hold X and a brass-and-oak dial unfolds from your wrist. Move your hand to a slot and let go to show it. A quick tap shows your last one again
  • 20 new stickers with sayings like "Well met!", "Huzzah!" and "Have at ye!" pop up on a plaque over your head for everyone to see
  • Start with four, earn eight more from Lantern Pass levels, and unlock six by doing great deeds, like felling the First King, reviving a friend or winning with nobody falling
  • Pick which eight sit on your dial at the new Emotes tab on the Robing Board, or check them on your wrist
  • Plaques now sit neatly on top of every hat, and grow a little when you're far away so friends across the hall can still read them

v0.17.0 · The King Crumbles Alpha

2026-11-22

  • Break the First King's knee and his whole lower leg falls off. Break an elbow and the forearm and fist come down with it
  • Broken joints leave glowing ember stumps, and the stumps are his new weak points
  • Take out a knee and he sinks down and keeps fighting on the stump. Take both and he fights on his knees
  • Lose him an arm and he swings with the other one
  • When he falls, he breaks into big stone chunks right where he went down

v0.16.0 · Fingertips and Firebeards Alpha

2026-11-20

  • Tap any menu, board or keypad with your fingertip, with either hand. The pointer ray still works for far boards
  • Beards now sit out in front of your chin. New: Long Full Beard, Forked Beard, Long Braid (Pass Lv 18), and 6 hair styles including Topknot, Curly Mop and a Mohawk (Pass Lv 12)
  • 19 hair colours, and your beard can have its own colour
  • Fireball Glove: a smaller splash that walls and raised shields now stop, throws go where your hand throws even while walking, and the gloved hand can still grab bows, levers and ropes
  • Laser Bow: quick shots fly like the wooden bow for 5% heat. Hold at full draw to charge a 2- or 3-damage laser bolt
  • When the First King kneels, jump at his flank or back and you're carried up onto his back

v0.15.0 · Relics of the Pit Alpha

2026-11-19

  • New in the Ember Pit: clear wave 5 to open the Relic Rack and take the Laser Bow or the Fireball Glove. One per player, and they stay in the Pit
  • The Laser Bow fires fast, flat energy bolts with no ammo. Watch the heat meter on the bow, and flick down or press the face button to vent
  • The Fireball Glove: hold grip to charge, then throw. A full charge explodes and knocks foes back
  • The First King's bronze bands now visibly break off, and when he kneels you can grab the glowing handholds and climb onto his back with your hands
  • The Pit is tougher: no healing during waves (rest at the breather or eat an apple), the gold turret fires a 3-bolt spray, and the gold archer now wears red and gold
  • Fixed: archers stacking on the same ledge spot, and a gate sound that looped through the First King's fight

v0.14.0 · Steam and Spray Alpha

2026-11-19

  • The First King now falls in one continuous motion: stagger, topple, crash, then he breaks apart. He no longer pops back to standing
  • The King now really swings at you when you're on the floor next to him, even with a sword and shield in hand
  • Steam vents scald every foe for a clear 6 seconds, glowing red the whole time
  • New elite in the Ember Pit: the Gilded Thornshot, a gold archer whose longer draw ends in a three-arrow spray
  • Pit turrets rise from stands above the spawn arches on three sides, and the Endless Pit lectern faces the gallery
  • The Swiftest Fellowships board in the hub is redone in the gold-framed Tally style, with names, times, dates and party size
  • Under the hood: much smaller scene files

v0.13.0 · The King Falls Alpha

2026-11-18

  • The First King now loses his armour bands as big stone chunks that crash, roll and lie in the hall, and on defeat he collapses into heavy rubble
  • He fights the whole hall, hurling rubble at the landing and balconies, and crushes the Gilded Hulker to gold dust in every intro
  • When he kneels, climb his back and strike the glowing neck rune. He holds still while you ride him and sets you down gently
  • The Ember Pit gate is now in the Main Hall beside the score board, and the Viewing Gallery has a stone balustrade, so the start stone is the only way down
  • Steam vents now scald every foe, turrets stand on their own towers, and a gold turret firing flaming bolts joins from wave 8
  • Gilded bats take two hits and fire twice, and the early Pit waves start tougher

v0.12.0 · The Ember Pit Alpha

2026-11-18

  • New: the Ember Pit, a wave arena behind the iron gate in the Practice Yard. Waves 1–15 are free, with bosses at waves 5, 10 and 15
  • Ring the brazier bell to skip the breather. Breathers heal you and stand fallen friends back up
  • The Pit board by the gate tracks your best runs on the Pit Masters and Free Pit ladders
  • New Robing items to earn in the Pit: the Pit Tabard, the Ember Crest and the Ashen Mantle
  • The Warden now wins back foes after you stop attacking them, Ironbacks included
  • The Gauntlet Drill pop-up boards all face the shooters now

v0.11.0 · Threat and Ruin Alpha

2026-11-18

  • Enemies now turn on whoever hurts them most. Hit a Hulker or Ironback a couple of times and it comes for you, with a head turn and a grunt as warning. The Warden only holds aggro while you aren't fighting
  • The Ironback's weak spot is just the glowing back vent now, and it sparks when you hit it
  • Key and seal locks show a faint ghost guide only while you hold the key near them
  • The First King's fire breath hurts when the flames actually reach you
  • Smash the First King's elbows and knees to knock off stone bands: he loses that arm's attacks or starts limping, and a glowing core opens up underneath
  • The railings in the King's hall line up, and the build number now shows in Credits and the wrist menu

v0.10.0 · The First King Alpha

2026-11-17

  • The Gallery of Kings now ends in the Hall of the First King: a giant stone king wakes, smashes his own statues and the Gilded Hulker, then turns on you
  • Strike his joints while they glow. Break a knee and he kneels, leaving the back of his neck open
  • Dodge his slam, sweep and stomp: step out, crouch, hide behind a pillar or climb a stair. The pillars crumble as the fight goes on
  • At half strength he flares his crown, throws rubble at archers on the balconies, and bats join the fight
  • Bring him down for coins, XP and his treasure hoard. The intro is shorter on repeat plays
  • Seated players: his sweep passes higher, so a seated crouch clears it
